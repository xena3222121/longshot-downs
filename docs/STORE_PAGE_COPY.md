## About This Game (full store page body)

```
[b]The tote board is up. Post time is close. Get your bet down.[/b]

Longshot Downs is a broadcast-style horse racing sim built around one thing: the feeling of watching a real simulcast betting parlor, not a menu with horses attached. Odds drift live on the board. Multiple tracks run their own independent post-time countdowns at once. Miss the window and the race goes off without you — exactly like the real thing.

[h2]Live multi-track simulcast[/h2]
Eighteen original tracks, each with its own layout, lighting, and mood — from tight bullring sprints to sweeping mile-and-a-quarter ovals, dirt and turf both. Watch one track full-screen or run up to four side by side, switching your attention (and your audio focus) wherever the action is.

[h2]Bet like it's the real thing[/h2]
Win, Place, Show, Exacta, Quinella, Trifecta, Superfecta, and Daily Double — eight bet types with real pari-mutuel payout math. Odds drift in the minutes before post as the "betting public" moves money, including the occasional late-money plunge on a horse nobody saw coming. A live tote board, a full odds board with jockey/form data, and a $2 payoff board after every race keep the broadcast feel honest all the way through.

[h2]Sixty horses, zero of them normal[/h2]
Build a bankroll backing "Frostbite Folly" or "Sir Poops-a-Lot" — a full sixty-horse roster with real jockeys, real form lines, and names that range from classic to completely unhinged. No two races feel the same: dynamic lane changes, mid-race surges, and a genuine shot at a photo finish every time.

[h2]Career mode[/h2]
Climb the ranks from Maiden Special Weight through Claiming, Allowance, Stakes, and all the way to Grade 1 — bigger purses, tougher fields, and a full stats page tracking your winningest horses, your best streak, and your worst nights. Eight Steam achievements and two leaderboards (peak bankroll, longest win streak) for anyone who wants to chase a number.

[h2]Built for a controller[/h2]
Full gamepad support, PlayStation and Xbox glyphs, rumble on the big moments. Settings for track theme, screen layout, and audio, all navigable without ever touching a mouse.

[b]No real money changes hands here.[/b] Every bet uses simulated in-game currency only — nothing can be bought, wagered, or withdrawn for real money, ever.
```
